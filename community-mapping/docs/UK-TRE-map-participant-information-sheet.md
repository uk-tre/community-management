## Key points

This section gives a brief overview to help you decide whether to take part. More detail follows.

- We are building an interactive map of the UK TRE community. We invite you to be part of it.
- Before you decide, please read this information. If anything is unclear, please ask.
- It is entirely your choice whether to take part, and you choose how you appear: not at all, internally only, publicly under a pseudonymised ID, or publicly under your name.
- You can change your mind at any time.
- The form to provide your data for the map takes about 5 minutes to complete and is pre-populated with the details we already hold.

## What is the project about?

We are mapping who the UK TRE community is: its members, the organisations they belong to, and the working groups and communities they take part in. Internally, this data will help the people who manage the community to build a data-informed community strategy, to report on our activities in a nuanced way, and to understand and support the community better over time. If you consent to being publicly identifiable on the map, it also makes it easier for peers to find your expertise and for you to connect with collaborators and opportunities tailored to your interests.

There are two versions of the map visualisation:

- A public map of the UK TRE community and its relevant groups and members, built only from data of people who have agreed to appear on it.
- An internal map, used by the community management team for strategy development and impact analysis.

You can see an [example of this style of map](http://tinyurl.com/turing-es-map).

## Why does it matter?

A map makes visible the breadth of TRE activity, and how its people and groups are connected. That helps members connect with each other and contribute to the UK TRE community and coordinate across groups. In this way the map becomes a shared asset, giving equitable access to knowledge about the community's structure and interconnectivity.

Internally, it helps us understand who is involved and how, so that knowledge about the community is transferable and reproducible. Information about engagement is described, not scored, and we do not use the map to rank people. We will also develop measures of community health based on network properties of the map, so that we can adjust how we engage with the community.

The project is carried out by the UK TRE Community Management Working Group, with RCM Cooperative, as part of our DARE UK funded programme for UK TRE. Internal data may be shared with selected partners (for example RCM Cooperative and DARE UK) in support of the project's mission, only where an explicit and approved data sharing contract is in place.

Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (MFT) has granted permission for you to be invited to take part in this service improvement project. MFT is the Data Controller.

## Why have I been asked to take part?

You are being invited to contribute to this map because we already hold your details from one or more of three sources: the UK TRE community's Slack workspace, the UK TRE Jisc mailing list, and the [public registry of DARE UK funded interest groups and working groups](https://dareuk.org.uk/community-groups/directory). The form is pre-populated with this data for you to validate. We would also like you to add any further metadata you wish to share to enrich the data, and to choose your desired consent level for visibility in the map.

## What will happen if I take part?

You will receive an email with a personal link to a short online form, and a personal ID (Baserow Unique User ID (UUID)). The form is pre-populated with the data we already hold for you. Please do not share the link with anyone: it is personal to you. The form asks you to:

- choose your level of consent;
- tell us your organisational affiliations, career stage and the type of role you hold in relation to TREs;
- optionally add public profile links (such as a profile page on your organisation's website, ORCID, GitHub and LinkedIn) and other networks you would like to connect on;
- tell us which TRE communities or working groups you lead or take part in;
- confirm that your data is correct, and the date you reviewed it.

If you received the invitation at more than one email address, you will have more than one Baserow UUID. This will occur when you have used different email addresses to register in one of our data holdings. You only need to complete the form once, but please paste all of the Baserow UUIDs sent to you into a single form. This tells us the addresses belong to you, and lets us link your records without sharing either address with the other.

There is no follow-up required. You can request your personal link at any time to update your data. We will also include a link to a blank data entry form on the public map, should new members wish to be added. You may be asked to review your data at future intervals (for example every two years), at which point your Baserow UUID and pre-populated form link will be emailed to you, unless you have opted out of future mapping (answered "none" to the consent question).

We will publicise the results of this mapping via Slack, Jisc, and in our reporting to DARE UK.

## Are there any benefits in my taking part?

There is no payment or reimbursement. The benefits of your participation are indirect: if you choose to be publicly identifiable, you become easier for peers to find, the map can help you find collaborators and opportunities tailored to your interests, and it will improve coordination across the TRE ecosystem. Your contribution also helps the community understand itself and shape its future activity.

If you choose Internal only or Public PSEUDONYMISED, your data still helps the UK TRE Community Management Working Group. It allows us to plan effectively and to advocate for the community.

## Are there any risks involved?

We do not anticipate significant risks. The main consideration is visibility. If you choose to be PSEUDONYMISED, information about your organisation, community group participation, career role type and career stage will appear on a public map. If you choose to be IDENTIFIABLE, your name and professional profile link will be visible alongside the above and public registry listings. You control this visibility through your consent choice, and you can change it later.

## What data will be collected?

You will be asked to provide or review the following data. All fields except name, email address and consent choice and review date are optional.

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your Baserow UUID, your consent choice, and the date you last reviewed your data
- Your organisational affiliations, career stage and type of role in relation to TREs
- Professional public profile links: organisation profile page, ORCID, GitHub, LinkedIn, other social networks
- The working groups and communities you lead or take part in
- Whether your address is on the Slack workspace or Jisc mailing list, and the groups listed for you in the DARE UK public directory
- A UUID key mapping connecting your MFT data to Baserow. A different (hashed) pseudonymised UUID is used in the visualisation if you choose to be pseudonymised. It is generated from your Baserow UUID using a salted one-way hash, so this UUID cannot be traced back to your Baserow or MFT UUID without a secret salt held privately by the project team. The same person receives the same hashed ID each time the map is updated.

We do not ask for protected characteristics. Please do not enter other people's details in free-text fields.

## How will my information and personal details be protected?

The data is collected under MFT approval (REC 23/NE/0076, DPIA B01917, ISO 27001 IS806270) and is retained for service improvement. It is archived in line with the policies and procedures of the [Manchester Clinical Data Science Unit](https://research.cmft.nhs.uk/innovation-at-mft/manchester-clinical-data-science-unit) (CDSU).

The data is used by the UK TRE Community Management Working Group and shared with selected partners (for example RCM Cooperative and DARE UK) in support of our mission, only where an explicit and approved data sharing contract is in place. Your data will never be sold.

The data you provide in the Baserow form and Kumu visualisation is pseudonymised, aggregated or already exists in public registries (for example ORCID). As such it is considered low risk. This data is not linked to your real identity (name) on any public map unless you give explicit consent to be publicly identifiable. Your email address will not be held outside of MFT infrastructure or appear on any public map.

Data will be stored on MFT infrastructure, in a dedicated private database hosted by Baserow on secure servers in Germany ([Baserow's Privacy Policy](https://baserow.io/privacy-policy)), and on private and public visualisations hosted by Kumu, a US-based service ([Kumu](https://www.kumu.io/privacy)['s Privacy Policy](https://www.kumu.io/privacy)). Kumu Inc. participates in the Data Privacy Framework, including the UK Extension, which the UK government recognises as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred to certified US organisations. Kumu has appointed EDPO UK Ltd as its UK representative under Article 27 of the UK GDPR.

Your responses are treated in the strictest confidence. It will not be possible to identify individuals in the dataset unless you have given explicit consent to be identifiable when outputs of this project are shared.

## Do I have to take part?

No. It is entirely up to you. Choosing "None" is a valid and complete consent choice. If you take part, indicating your consent on the form confirms you agree to take part on the basis of the consent statement below. Consent does not commit you to anything you do not wish to do.

## What happens if I change my mind?

You are free to withdraw, or to change your consent level, at any time, in part or in full, without penalty and without giving a reason. Use your personal link to update your choice, or contact the project team (details below).

## What will happen to the results of the project?

Results are used for service improvement: to run the map, to understand the needs of the community, and to adjust how we engage with it. Outputs will not directly identify you without your explicit consent to be identifiable.

## Data Protection Privacy Notice

This sheet tells you what data is collected and how it is used. Please ask the project team if you have any questions.

### How will we use information about you?

Personal data is used only for the purposes of this service improvement project. Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (MFT) is the Data Controller. Where possible your data is pseudonymised, so that people who do not need to know who you are cannot see your name or contact details. We use the minimum personal data needed to achieve the project's aims.

### What are your choices about how your information is used?

- You can stop taking part at any time, without giving a reason, by changing your consent level or contacting the project team.
- If you choose "None", we keep only the minimum data needed for a suppression list, so that you are not contacted or processed again.

### How long do we keep your data?

We are obliged to retain this service improvement data for up to 10 years after the project is completed. Pseudonymised data may be retained indefinitely so that the team can continue service improvement practice and confirm the conclusions of activities. Consistent with our data retention policy, we retain consent records for as long as we hold information about a data subject, and for 10 years for published outputs.
